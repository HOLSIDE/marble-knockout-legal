# Privacy policy

*Last updated: 12 November 2026* · [Versione italiana](privacy.html)

This policy explains what personal data the **Marble Knockout** app (Android, iOS and web), developed by **Holside**, processes, why, who it is shared with and what your rights are under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR").

## 1. Controller

Holside — data controller: Davide Travaglini.

Contact for any privacy request: **info@holside.it**

## 2. What data we process

The only personal data we need is your **email**. Everything else is the game.

| Data | When | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Email and password (the password is only stored as a one-way hash) | On sign-up | Create and protect your account |
| Apple or Google account identifier and email | If you sign in with Apple or Google | Password-free sign-in |
| Date you confirmed being at least 14 | On sign-up | Show that the minimum age was checked. We do **not** ask for your date of birth |
| Username, bio, profile picture if any | Profile | Let other players recognise you. You can use a nickname |
| Marble collection, matches, tournaments, trades, wishlist, friendships, notifications and this month's usage counters | Using the app | Run the game and the monthly limits |
| Device token for push notifications | Only if you turn notifications on | Tell you about matches, trades and friend requests |

We do **not** collect location, contacts, photos or payment details. There are no purchases, ads or analytics. The camera is only used to read a friend's QR code; no image is stored or sent.

## 3. Legal bases

- **Performance of a contract** (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): account, game, notifications.
- **Legal obligation** (Art. 6(1)(c) and Art. 8 GDPR): minimum age check.
- **Legitimate interest** (Art. 6(1)(f)): security of the service and abuse prevention.

## 4. Who sees your data

- **Other players**: username, bio, profile picture, stats and, if your collection is public, the marbles you own. Your email is never shown. You can make your collection private in Settings.
- **Providers processing data on our behalf** (processors):
  - **Supabase Inc.**: database, authentication and storage, on servers in the European Union;
  - **Expo / 650 Industries Inc.** (United States), **Apple** and **Google**: delivery of push notifications.
- **Apple and Google** as sign-in providers, if you choose to use them.

When data reaches providers in the United States, the transfer is covered by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or by the Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission.

We do not sell or hand over your data to anyone.

## 5. How long

- Account and game data are kept for as long as you have an account.
- Read notifications are deleted after 90 days; unused push tokens after 180 days.
- When you delete your account, your login, email and age confirmation date are deleted and your profile is anonymised. Only anonymous data needed to keep other players' match and trade history intact remains.

## 6. Children

The app is for people **aged 14 and over** (the threshold set in Italy by Art. 2-quinquies of the Privacy Code): you confirm it with a checkbox when you sign up. If we find that an account belongs to someone under 14, we delete it.

## 7. Your rights

At any time you can:
- **download a copy of your data** and **delete your account** in the app's Settings (or by following the steps on the [Delete your account](delete-account.html#en) page);
- ask for access, rectification, restriction, objection or portability by writing to **info@holside.it**;
- lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority ([garanteprivacy.it](https://www.garanteprivacy.it)) or the authority of your country.

We reply within 30 days.

## 8. Security

All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). On your phone the sign-in session is kept in the system's secure storage (Keychain on iOS, Keystore on Android). Database rules limit server-side access so that each player only sees what they are allowed to.

## 9. Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way we will tell you in the app. The date at the top shows the latest version.
